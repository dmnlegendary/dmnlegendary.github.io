IT Support & Quality Assurance

Instituto Politécnico Nacional (IPN) | Apr 2025 – Sep 2025 | Mexico City (Hybrid)

During my social service at the Instituto Politécnico Nacional, I worked as an IT Helper in a hybrid environment. This role allowed me to bridge the gap between technical infrastructure and end-user experience, ensuring that educational and administrative processes ran smoothly with minimal downtime.

Key Responsibilities & Impact

Working in IT support required a strong methodological approach to troubleshooting, as well as the ability to adapt to different user needs across both physical and remote environments.

Technical Troubleshooting

Delivered comprehensive technical support and online assistance. I was responsible for systematically diagnosing and resolving software and network issues, ensuring the reliability of the institution's systems.

User Communication

A major part of my role involved translating complex technical diagnostics into clear, actionable steps for non-technical users. This improved resolution times and empowered users to handle minor issues independently.

Quality Assurance

Conducted rigorous quality assurance (QA) reviews on multimedia resources. By verifying the accuracy and functionality of course data and educational materials, I helped maintain a high standard for the institution's digital learning platforms.

Key Takeaways

This experience strengthened my foundational knowledge in networking and systems administration while significantly developing my soft skills. Learning how to communicate technical problems to end-users has been invaluable for my subsequent software development projects, where understanding the user's perspective is critical.
